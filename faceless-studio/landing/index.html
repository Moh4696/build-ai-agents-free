Questions

What do I need?

A computer with the latest Chrome or Edge. Phones and tablets aren't supported for exporting yet. The free AI voice downloads about 90 MB the first time you use it.

Can I use the videos commercially and for clients?

Yes. The built-in music is generated for you and the captions and backgrounds are yours. If you upload music or images, make sure you have the rights to them.

Does the AI voice speak other languages?

The free voices are English. For other languages, use Pro with your own ElevenLabs account, or upload a voiceover. Captions work in any language.

How many computers can I use?

Your license works on up to 3 browsers.
