# Done-For-You Video Service Kit

Use Faceless Studio (in Studio mode, everything unlocked) to make short videos for local businesses and creators who don't want to be on camera. This is the fastest way to earn with $0 upfront: you get paid before you spend anything.

> **About prices:** the figures below are a starting point, not researched market rates. Before you quote, check what freelancers charge for "short-form video content" in your area (search Fiverr and Upwork, and ask local agencies). Then adjust.

## 2. Who to contact first

Businesses that need regular content and hate filming: dentists, estate agents, gyms and personal trainers, cafés, salons, accountants, local trades (roofers, plumbers), coaches, and online course creators.

Find them on Instagram, Facebook and Google Maps. Look for accounts that post rarely or post only flat images.

---

## 3. The free sample (your best sales tool)

Before you pitch, **make them one free video**:
1. Grab their logo and colours from their website or Instagram.
2. Write a 20-second script about something on their site (an offer, a tip, an FAQ).
3. In Faceless Studio: **Brand tab → save as kit** (use their business name), then make the video.
4. Send it with the message below. It takes 10 minutes per prospect.

---

## 4. Outreach messages

**Instagram / Facebook DM**
> Hi {Name}, I made this short video for {Business} using your logo and colours. No catch, it's yours to post. I make videos like this every week for businesses that don't have time to film. Want me to send a few more ideas?

**Email**
> Subject: Made a video for {Business}
>
> Hi {Name},
>
> I put together a 20-second video for {Business}, attached and free to use. It's the kind of short, captioned video that does well on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
>
> I make these for local businesses every month: scripts, branding, everything done for you, from {price}/month for 8 videos.
>
> Want me to make 3 more for this month as a trial?
>
> {Your name}

**Follow-up (3 days later)**
> Hi {Name}, just checking you got the video. Happy to make one about {specific thing from their site} if that's more useful.

Aim for **20 messages a day**. Expect most people not to reply; that's normal.

---

## 5. Client onboarding form (copy into Google Forms or Tally, both free)

1. Business name and website
2. Instagram / TikTok / YouTube handles
3. Upload your logo (PNG if possible)
4. Brand colours (or "use my website colours")
5. Who are your customers?
6. Three things you want people to know about you
7. Offers or promotions coming up this month
8. Topics to avoid
9. Tone: friendly / professional / funny / inspiring
10. Voice preference: female, male, no voice (captions + music only)
11. Who approves the videos, and their email

---

## 6. Monthly delivery workflow (about 2–3 hours for 16 videos)

1. Write the month's scripts in one document. One idea per video, 40–80 words. Ask Claude to draft them from the client's onboarding answers.
2. In Faceless Studio, pick the client's **brand kit**.
3. Paste all scripts separated by `===` and tick **Batch mode**.
4. Click **Make my video**, then **Download all (.zip)**.
5. Send via Google Drive with a short list of suggested captions and hashtags.
6. Make revisions, then invoice next month up front.

---

## 7. What to say if asked "is this AI?"

Be honest: *"I use AI tools for the voice and editing, and I write and check every script for your business."* Many platforms ask you to label AI-generated content. Follow their rules when you post for clients.
