# 20 daily posts (TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels)

Post one a day for 20 days. They're made with Faceless Studio, so every post also proves the product works.

**Audience:** UK tradespeople. Your bio link goes to the waitlist page (`waitlist.html`), which offers both "done for you" and "do it yourself".

**Caption to paste under each post:** the title, then *"Made with AI in 2 minutes, no filming. Link in bio."* Add 3–5 hashtags such as #tradesman #roofer #plumber #smallbusinessuk #electrician.

**Honesty:** don't claim results you haven't had (e.g. "this got me 10 jobs"). These are ideas and tips, not case studies.

To render: `node tools/render-scripts.mjs validation/tool/daily-posts.md --out renders/posts`
