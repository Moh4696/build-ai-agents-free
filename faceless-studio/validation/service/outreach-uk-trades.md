# Outreach kit: done-for-you videos for UK tradespeople

**Goal of this test:** find out whether at least one tradesperson will **pay** for a month of videos. Replies and compliments are nice, but only payment counts as a pass.

---

## 1. Finding prospects (about 30 minutes a day)

Look for trades businesses that are active enough to care, but post rarely or only post flat photos:
- **Google Maps:** search "roofer near [town]", "plumber [town]", and so on. Open their website and social links.
- **Instagram / Facebook:** search hashtags like #roofer[town], #plumber[town], #[town]builder.
- **Directory listings** (Checkatrade, MyBuilder, Rated People): use these only to find business names, then contact them through their own website or social page.

Log every business in `prospect-tracker.xlsx` **before** you message them.

## 2. How to contact them (UK rules)

> Not legal advice. Under UK PECR, cold marketing emails to **sole traders and partnerships** generally need their prior consent. Most small trades businesses are one of these.

- ✅ **Instagram / Facebook DMs** and **website contact forms**: the main channels for this test.
- ✅ Emails to **limited companies** at a business address are generally allowed, as long as you say who you are and offer an easy opt-out.
- ❌ Don't buy email lists, don't email sole traders out of the blue, and don't spam Facebook groups (check each group's rules first).
- Always respect "no thanks". Mark them in the tracker and never contact them again.

## 3. The free personalised sample (your best tool)

For each prospect, before messaging:
1. Grab their logo and colours from their website or Instagram.
2. Pick a script from `trades-scripts.md` that matches their trade. Add a last line: *"Need a hand in [Town]? Message [Business name]."*
3. In Faceless Studio: **Brand** tab, then **Save as kit** (use their name). Make the video and download the MP4.
4. Send it **privately** with the message below. **Never post it publicly or tag them**; it's their branding.

About 10 minutes per prospect once you're used to it.

## 4. Messages

### First message (DM or contact form)
> Hi [Name], I made this short video for [Business] using your logo and colours. No catch, it's yours to use if you like it.
>
> I make videos like this for trades businesses who don't have time to film: tips customers actually search for, in your branding, ready to post.
>
> Would a month of these be useful? Happy to send a couple more ideas first.
>
> (If this isn't for you, just say and I won't message again.)

### Follow-up (3–4 days later, once only)
> Hi [Name], just checking you saw the video I sent. I can also make one about [something specific from their site, e.g. "your boiler servicing"] if that's more useful.

### If they reply "how much?" (alternate prices: see section 5)
> It's **£[249 or 449] a month** for **8 videos**: scripts written for you, your logo and colours, captions, voiceover and music, plus 1 round of changes. Month to month, cancel anytime. First month paid up front.
>
> If you'd like to try it first, I can do **3 videos free** this week so you can see how they perform.

### If they agree to the free trial
> Great. I'll send 3 videos by [day]. Anything you'd like them to focus on, such as a service you want more work for, or a common question customers ask?

### After the trial (2–3 days after posting)
> How did the videos go? If you'd like to keep going, I can start your first month on [date]. 8 videos, £[price]. I'll send a simple invoice.

## 5. Price test

You're testing **two prices** to learn what people accept. Both are guesses, not researched market rates.
- **Odd-numbered prospects** in the tracker (1, 3, 5…) are quoted **£249/month**.
- **Even-numbered prospects** are quoted **£449/month**.
- Record the price quoted and their reaction in the tracker every time, even "too expensive".

After about 15 price conversations, the tracker shows which price gets more yeses.

## 6. Common objections and honest answers

| They say | You can say |
|---|---|
| "Is this AI?" | "Yes, I use AI for the voice and editing, and I write and check every script for your business." |
| "I get enough work from word of mouth." | "That's great. These help people who hear about you check you out online and see you know your stuff." |
| "Too expensive." | Thank them, ask what would feel fair, record the answer, and don't argue. |
| "Will it get me jobs?" | "I can't promise that, and anyone who does is guessing. That's why the first 3 are free, so you can see how they do." |

## 7. Getting paid (no payment platform needed)

- Send a simple invoice (free tools: Google Docs or Wave) with **bank transfer** details, or a **PayPal** payment request.
- First month **up front**, before you start the 8 videos.
- Keep it month to month with a written summary: what's included, 1 round of changes, cancel anytime with notice before the next month.
- Keep records of income for tax. Register for Self Assessment with HMRC if you start earning.

## 8. Daily routine (about 1.5–2 hours)

1. Find and log 20 prospects (30 min).
2. Make free samples for the most promising 3–5 (40 min).
3. Send messages and follow-ups, then update the tracker (30 min).
4. Post today's video from `../tool/daily-posts.md` (10 min).
